    Sources & caveats

    • Official underlying data: Virginia Department of Elections (ELECT) Daily Absentee List. It's sold ($76 per election), not posted, so we use two sources built from it:
    • 2024, 2026 daily: EPEC / Digital Poll Watchers DAL metrics, digitalpollwatchers.org/files/DALTracker/
    • 2022 daily (and 2026 cross-check): VPAP, Early Voting in Virginia, vpap.org/elections/early-voting
    • Secondary sources. Both are built from the official ELECT file. The 2026 totals match within a few votes (EPEC 635,348 vs VPAP 635,329 for Oct 15).
    • Virginia has no party registration and doesn't collect race on registration, so there are no party or race charts.
    • Counts are ballots "countable" so far: early in person ("on machine") plus mail ballots received and marked. A file pulled each morning is dated the previous day. The 2022 series is VPAP's daily total, with no in-person/mail split by day.

    Law changes that affect comparisons

    Rule changes between cycles can shift how (not just how many) people vote early. Keep these in mind when comparing to past years.

    • 2020 · HB 1 / SB 111: no-excuse absentee voting, including 45 days of early in-person voting, began in 2020. In 2018 voters needed an excuse, so 2018 early voting (about 345,000 in total) isn't comparable day by day. HB 1 (2020)
    • 2020-21: prepaid return postage, drop-off boxes, a permanent absentee list and pre-processing of mail ballots were added; mail ballots postmarked by Election Day and received by noon on the third day after are counted. ELECT: early and absentee voting
