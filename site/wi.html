    Sources & caveats

    • 2024: Wisconsin Elections Commission daily county absentee reports, e.g. County Absentee Counts as of October 18, 2024.csv
    • 2022: WEC daily absentee report attachments via the Internet Archive, e.g. Oct 21, 2022 county report
    • 2022/2024 statewide series compiled from those files by ElectIndex/26_early_vote (spot-checked: Oct 18, 2024 = 305,344; Oct 21, 2022 = 225,330)
    • 2026: civicAPI early-vote tracker (free public API from WEC county reports)
    • 2026 is secondary. WEC pages sit behind a bot check; 2026 numbers come from civicAPI, which republishes WEC's county reports. Past years are WEC's own files.
    • No 2018 series yet. WEC's 2018 daily reports weren't found online in a machine-readable form.
    • Wisconsin has no party registration and WEC doesn't publish race, so there are no party or race charts. Counts are dated by report date (each report is generated at 7:30 a.m.).

    Law changes that affect comparisons

    Rule changes between cycles can shift how (not just how many) people vote early. Keep these in mind when comparing to past years.

    • No major change to absentee rules since 2018 for how ballots are cast: in-person absentee runs the two weeks before Election Day (Oct 20–Nov 1, 2026), and ballots must arrive by 8 p.m. Election Day. Wis. Stat. § 6.86
    • 2024 · Supreme Court of Wisconsin, Priorities USA v. WEC: allowed drop boxes again (at clerks' discretion) (they had been barred in 2022 by Teigen v. WEC). That affects 2024 and 2026 vs 2022. 2024 WI 32 opinion
