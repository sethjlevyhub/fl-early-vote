Mostly secondary sources. The Secretary of State's site blocks automated downloads, so 2026 statewide numbers come from civicAPI, and 2018–2024 statewide party leads are figures Jon Ralston stated on The Nevada Independent's early-voting blog or on X (marked †). Clark County mail ballots (about 70% of the state's votes) come from the county's official files for every year.

What the numbers say

    Clark County mail ballots received, cumulative (official)

    Clark County Election Department cumulative mail-ballot files, counted by the date each ballot was received (undeliverable ballots excluded). Clark mailed ballots earlier in 2026 (about Oct 8) than in 2024 (Oct 10) or 2022 (Oct 13), so early 2026 gains partly reflect timing.

    Statewide ballot lead, Democrats minus Republicans †

    Ballots cast by registered Democrats minus registered Republicans (mail + in person). 2026: civicAPI daily. 2018–2024: figures Jon Ralston stated on the date shown (secondary; a few are "about" or "just under").

    2026 statewide ballots by party †

    civicAPI daily totals; "Other" includes nonpartisan and minor parties.

    Total ballots cast, statewide †

    2026 civicAPI; 2022 and 2024 are totals Ralston stated on the blog.

    Sources & caveats

    • 2026 statewide: civicAPI NV early-vote history (free public API built from state/county data; also compiled by ElectIndex)
    • Clark County: Mail Ballot Data Files: mbreq26GE.zip, mbreq24G.zip, mbreq22G.zip (aggregated to daily totals; no voter-level data published here)
    • 2024: Jon Ralston, The early voting blog, 2024 (The Nevada Independent) and @RalstonReports
    • 2022: The early voting blog, 2022
    • 2018: @RalstonReports posts on X (the 2018 blog is no longer online), e.g. final 2018 early/mail lead
    • Every Ralston figure with its date, quote and link: nv-data.csv
    • Past statewide totals are sparse. Ralston mostly reported the party lead, not the total; no 18-day-out statewide total exists for 2022 or 2024, and 2018 has only party leads and Clark firewalls.
    • Ralston's numbers are as posted by the state that day (they lag the county's receive dates). Clark official counts are by receive date, so the two can't be matched exactly.
    • Nevada does not record race on voter registration, so there is no race chart.
    • In-person early voting runs Oct 17–30, 2026; the numbers so far are almost all mail.

    Law changes that affect comparisons

    Rule changes between cycles can shift how (not just how many) people vote early. Keep these in mind when comparing to past years.

    • 2021 · AB 321: every active registered voter is mailed a ballot unless they opt out (first used for all voters in 2020 under emergency law AB 4). In 2018 voters had to request a mail ballot, so 2018 mail totals are not comparable. NRS 293
    • Mail ballot deadline: postmarked by Election Day and received by 5 p.m. on the fourth day after (same in 2022, 2024 and 2026; a 2025 bill that would have changed drop-box rules, AB 499, was vetoed). SOS regulation R088-25
