    Sources & caveats

      • No party or race data. Michigan has no party registration, and the dashboard publishes counts only.
      • 2024 was a presidential year. Midterm turnout is lower, so a gap against 2024 is expected. 2022 (midterm) is the closer comparison, but only three rounded press-release figures exist and their exact as-of times aren't stated (taken as the release date).
      • Permanent absentee list: Proposal 2 (2022) let voters join a permanent mail-ballot list. By 2026 most requests (~95%) come from it, so ballots are sent to many voters automatically. That inflates "requested/sent" relative to 2022.
      • The dashboard only keeps the current cycles (2024 general, 2026 primary and general). The 2024 series starts Sept 26, 2024; 2026 starts Sept 19, 2026. One 2024 value (Oct 9) is a clear dashboard glitch (returned briefly showed 1.20M, then 468K the next day) and is dropped.
      • Counts are as recorded in the Qualified Voter File by local clerks, dated by the dashboard's count date. Small decreases occur when ballots are spoiled or rejected. "Total" = absentee returned + early in-person; voters who had an absentee ballot but voted early in person are counted under early in-person.
