What this tracks

  • Ballots cast before Election Day (mail/absentee ballots returned, and early in-person votes where the state reports them) for the Nov 3, 2026 general election in 17 states.
  • Same days out: every comparison lines up 2026 with 2018 (Nov 6), 2022 (Nov 8) and 2024 (Nov 5) at the same number of days before Election Day. Where a past cycle has no figure for that exact day, the nearest day is used and marked (lighter bar, asterisk or note).
  • Party split means the registered party of voters who have already cast ballots, from state voter records. It is not a count of votes for candidates. States without party registration (GA, MI, MN, OH, TX, VA, WI) show no party split.
  • Race and age are shown wherever the state collects them (North Carolina's file; Georgia via georgiavotes.com, marked secondary).
  • Data as of: the bar at the top of each page shows the date (and time, when the source carries one) of that state's latest data. It is written into each data file by the daily build. When a state hasn't updated on its usual schedule the bar turns amber and says how many days old the data is. "Checked" times are when this tracker downloaded the data, not when the state posted it.
  • Numbers are not a forecast. Early ballots lean toward whichever party's voters prefer voting early, and that varies by state and year. Polls are not used anywhere on this site.

Labels

  • † Secondary source: a number compiled by a tracker, journalist, university or nonprofit from official data, used when the state doesn't post the file publicly or blocks automated downloads. Each one is named on the chart and in the state's source list, and spot checks against official figures are noted where they exist.
  • Modeled or proxy figures are labeled as such: anything this tracker estimates instead of counting directly (Virginia's locality-lean groups and lean estimate; Ohio's party by last primary voted) is named a proxy or model estimate on the chart itself, kept separate from official counts, and never called registered party.
  • Rounded or approximate figures (for example "287k" as posted, or approximate dates) are drawn as hollow dots or marked approximate.
  • Every number traces to a source listed on its state page and to a row in that state's CSV data file (for example data.csv for Florida, nc-data.csv for North Carolina).
