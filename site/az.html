Limited comparisons. Arizona has no statewide official daily early-ballot report. Maricopa County (about 60% of voters) publishes a daily count, but only for the current election, and no archived same-day counts for 2018, 2022 or 2024 could be found. Past-cycle figures below are scattered official counts quoted in news reports on different days, so this page shows them on the timeline but does not compute percent changes.

    Sources & caveats

      • Two different Maricopa counts. The county's open-data layer counts ballots "in hand" (received, before signature checks): 178,069 on Oct 16. A tracker built from each county's daily early-ballot file (Stealth Analytics) shows 99,625 Maricopa returns that day. The 2022 and 2024 news figures came from the county's daily return files, so they probably match the smaller measure better. Treat the 2026-vs-past gap on the chart with caution.
      • Statewide 2026 (unofficial): Stealth Analytics, compiling each county's daily file, reports 165,881 returned of 3,245,843 issued across all 15 counties as of Oct 16. Some small counties' files are a few days old. It's shown for context only.
      • 2018: no same-period official count found.
      • 2024 was a presidential year. Early voting and ballot mailing start 27 days before Election Day (Oct 7 in 2026, per the AZ SOS calendar), so the calendars line up across cycles.
      • The county's party-specific layers in the same open-data account still show the July 2026 primary, so 2026 party splits aren't available from an official public source yet.

      Law changes that affect comparisons

      Rule changes between cycles can shift how (not just how many) people vote early. Keep these in mind when comparing to past years.

      • 2021 · SB 1485 (Laws 2021, ch. 359): renamed the Permanent Early Voting List the Active Early Voting List. Voters who don't vote an early ballot in two consecutive election cycles can be removed after a notice. 2024 and 2026 are the first cycles that count, and the first notices go out by Jan 15, 2027, so no one has been removed yet and the list sizes in 2022, 2024 and 2026 aren't affected.
