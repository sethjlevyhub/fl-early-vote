    Sources & caveats

    • 2026: civicAPI early-vote tracker (free public API from the MN SOS weekly absentee data)
    • 2024: MN SOS press release, Oct 17, 2024
    • 2022: MN SOS, Sep 30, 2022; KSTP, Oct 18, 2022; Star Tribune, Oct 28, 2022; SOS page via Internet Archive (Oct 13, compiled by ElectIndex)
    • 2018: MinnPost chart of SOS weekly totals
    • Weekly, not daily. The SOS updates once a week (Thursdays). 2026 points are dated by the first day the update showed up in civicAPI, so they may be a day late. Comparisons use the nearest past point within 3 days and say which day.
    • Secondary sources. The SOS site is behind a bot check. 2026 comes from civicAPI; 2018 dates are approximate (MinnPost labels weeks, not days).
    • No party registration and no race data in Minnesota's absentee statistics.

    Law changes that affect comparisons

    Rule changes between cycles can shift how (not just how many) people vote early. Keep these in mind when comparing to past years.

    • 2023 · HF 3 (Laws 2023, ch. 34): permanent absentee voter list, effective June 1, 2024. HF 3
    • 2023 · HF 1830 (Laws 2023, ch. 62): voting directly into a tabulator ("direct balloting", later "early voting") expanded from the last 7 days to the last 18 days before Election Day, with required weekend hours. 2022 and 2018 had the 7-day window. LWV Minnesota summary · Minn. Stat. 203B
    • Minnesota's absentee window is 46 days (unchanged): voting started Sept 18, 2026. Ballots must arrive by 8 p.m. Election Day. MN SOS: vote early by mail
