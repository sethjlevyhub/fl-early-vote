Seth Levy Errand Assistant – SMS Privacy Policy

Seth Levy operates the Seth Levy Errand Assistant, an automated notification service sending errand-status and reminder alerts from (865) 391-8971 to two opted-in household recipients, Seth Levy and Kelly Levy.

Message frequency varies (about 1-10 per day). Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages; reply HELP for help.
