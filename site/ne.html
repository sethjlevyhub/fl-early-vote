    Sources & caveats

    • 2026 daily (secondary †): civicAPI Nebraska tracker (returned, in person, requested; county files by region). Party = Democratic, Republican, and "Other" (nonpartisan plus minor parties).
    • 2022 same-day and NE-2 district figures (secondary †): charts posted on X by @NE2EarlyVote ("Nebraska Election Data"), built from the state files: statewide, Oct 16, NE-2, Oct 16, NE-2, Oct 15, NE-2 independents. Numbers were copied by hand from their charts. They post through-dates; this page shifts them one day to the state file date to match civicAPI. Their full tracker is now public: @ne2earlyvote's Nebraska Early Vote Tracker (built from the official Secretary of State early-voting lists: 2020, 2022 and 2024 finals and 2026 daily files). It is also the source of the ballots-sent figures above.
    • Official (Nebraska Secretary of State): Oct 10, 2022 release (164,611 ballots requested and sent), Oct 29, 2024 release (363,629 sent; 249,235 accepted incl. 46,459 in person), Nov 4, 2024 release (332,455 cast); canvass books 2018, 2022, 2024; voter registration statewide/county and by congressional district, Oct 1, 2026.
    • News (secondary †): AP, Nov 2018 (2018 requests); KETV, Oct 8, 2024 and WOWT, Nov 5, 2022 (Douglas County); KOLN, Oct 15, 2026 (Lancaster County).
    • All rows: ne-data.csv
    • No official daily figures. The Secretary of State sells its early-voting file and doesn't post daily statistics, and the county sites (Douglas, Lancaster) don't post daily counts either. Official numbers come only from occasional press releases and the final canvass. This tracker watches the SOS releases page for new figures.
    • The state file can lag the counties. On Oct 15, Lancaster County's election commissioner said his office had sent 83,000 ballots and about half had come back. The state-file figures show 38,177 requested and 15,773 returned there as of the Oct 16 file. Statewide 2026 totals here may undercount, and it isn't known whether the 2022 files lagged the same way.
    • Same-day comparisons: 2022 only, from @NE2EarlyVote, 19–27 days out so far. 2018 and 2024 have official or news checkpoints 7, 5 and 1 days out, which 2026 reaches Oct 27–Nov 2.
    • Party: "Other" = nonpartisan plus Libertarian, Legal Marijuana NOW and other parties. Nebraska's voter registration doesn't ask for race, so there is no race chart.
    • Senate race: Osborn is an independent, so registered Democrats and Republicans don't line up with his race. Nonpartisan and minor-party voters are 22.5% of registered voters statewide (SOS, Oct 1, 2026).

    Law changes that affect comparisons

    Rule changes between cycles can shift how (not just how many) people vote early.

    • Voter ID (Initiative 432, 2022; first used in 2024): voters requesting an early ballot must give a driver's license or state ID number, a copy of a photo ID, or a reasonable-impediment certificate. 2024 was the first general election under it, so 2022 and 2018 requests had no ID step. KETV, 2024; Douglas County Election Commission
    • All-mail precincts: counties may mail every voter a ballot in small precincts or whole counties. Those ballots are counted separately in the canvass (21,400 in 2018, 38,525 in 2022, 46,417 in 2024) and arrive in batches, which can move the statewide party split. @NE2EarlyVote reports that the 2026 all-mail ballots went out about five days earlier than in past years (†, not confirmed officially).
    • Calendar (unchanged): mail ballots go out 35 days before the election, early in-person voting at county offices starts 30 days before, and mail requests close the second Friday before Election Day (Oct 23, 2026). Nebraska SOS
