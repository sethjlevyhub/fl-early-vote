    Data coverage & gaps

    Sources & caveats

      • No party data. Texas has no party registration, and SOS turnout reports carry no party or race. Party estimates (e.g., by primary-vote history) would need the voter-level rosters joined to a voter file. Not done here.
      • Before early voting opens, the 2026 mail count is incomplete. On the new SOS reporting system (Civix, used since 2025), only a minority of counties had posted mail-ballot counts as of the latest report (e.g., Bexar, El Paso, Hidalgo near zero). Pre-Oct 19 comparisons reflect county reporting, not voter behavior.
      • 2018: SOS published daily turnout only for the 30 counties with the most registered voters, and only from the first day of early voting (no pre-EV mail series). Statewide daily reporting began in 2020.
      • Figures are unofficial, as reported by counties to the SOS; counties revise earlier days, and the latest capture of each report date is used. Mail = mail ballots received (accepted counts aren't reported). 2018 SOS pages show a 10-vote gap between the county rows and their own total on Oct 25. County rows are used.
      • Report dates are data dates (votes through the close of that day). Weekend reports exist; Sunday in-person hours are limited.
