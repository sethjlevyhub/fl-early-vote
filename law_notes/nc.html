Law changes that affect comparisons

North Carolina's rules changed in 2023–2024, so 2018 and 2022 were run under different rules than 2026.

  • 2023 · Senate Bill 747 (S.L. 2023-140): mail ballots must arrive by 7:30 p.m. on Election Day; the old 3-day grace period for postmarked ballots ended (military/overseas excepted). Same-day registrants during early voting now cast a "retrievable" ballot that can be pulled if address verification fails. NC General Assembly bill summary · bill text
  • Photo ID has been required since the 2024 primaries (not in place in 2018 or 2022). NC State Board of Elections
