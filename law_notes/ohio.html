Law changes that affect comparisons

Ohio's rules changed after 2022, which helps explain why 2026 shows far more in-person early voting and fewer mail ballots than past years.

  • 2023 · House Bill 458 (in effect April 7, 2023): only one ballot drop box per county, at the board of elections; the deadline to request a mail ballot moved up to 7 days before Election Day; mailed ballots had to arrive within 4 days after Election Day (down from 10); and early voting on the Monday before Election Day was eliminated (those hours moved earlier). Photo ID also became required. Ohio SOS · ACLU of Ohio summary
  • 2025 · Senate Bill 293 (in effect March 20, 2026): mail ballots must now be received by 7:30 p.m. on Election Day; the post-election grace period for postmarked ballots is gone (except military/overseas). 2026 is the first general election under this rule. Legislative Service Commission analysis · Butler County BOE
  • So 2018 and 2022 had more drop boxes, later mail deadlines, and the extra Monday; the 2026 "in-person up, mail down" shift partly reflects these rules, not just enthusiasm.
