Law changes that affect comparisons

Iowa shortened its early-voting window in 2021, so 2018 had a longer runway than 2022 and 2026.

  • 2021 · Senate File 413: early in-person voting and mailing of absentee ballots now start 20 days before Election Day (was 29); absentee ballots must be received by the county auditor when polls close on Election Day; counties can't mail absentee request forms unless the voter asks.
